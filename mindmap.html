AI Risk Lifecycle Regulation & Compliance Monitoring & Assurance
Architecture Authority Breakdown:
• LLM: Helps interpret language and communicate parameters.
• Policy-as-code: Decides eligibility / review requirements via static matching routes.
• Calculator: Determines the financial value amount explicitly.
Execution: We did not remove the LLM. We removed the LLM from being the final authority.
