# Bereavement Support Policy — POL-BEREAVE-001

## Conduct principles
Bereavement-related requests must be handled with empathy, clarity, and care.

## Completed travel
If travel has already been completed, standard automated refund is not available.

Manual review is required if:
- customer was given incorrect or conflicting information by an official channel
- customer reasonably relied on that information
- request involves bereavement, vulnerability, or complaint risk

## Prior misinformation
If a customer says a chatbot, employee, website, email, or official company channel gave incorrect or conflicting information, the AI system must:
- retrieve prior communication if available
- treat it as customer-reliance evidence
- not treat it as policy
- escalate to manual review
- avoid automatic denial or automatic refund
