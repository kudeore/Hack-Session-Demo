# Refund Policy — POL-REFUND-001

## Auto-refund eligibility
Automated refund preparation is allowed only if all are true:
- customer identity is verified
- booking was made directly
- travel has not started
- fare type is flexible
- refund amount is below 500 currency units
- refund goes to the original payment method
- no manual review trigger exists
- no prior misinformation flag exists

## Manual review triggers
Manual review is mandatory if:
- customer mentions bereavement, illness, hardship, vulnerability, or distress
- customer claims a chatbot, employee, website, or official channel promised a refund
- travel is completed or partially flown
- booking was made through a third party
- refund amount is above 500 currency units
- policy uncertainty or conflict exists
- customer mentions complaint, regulator, legal, or media escalation

## Prohibited automated actions
The AI system must not:
- promise refund approval before policy and eligibility checks
- treat previous chatbot output as policy
- execute refund when manual review is required
- deny a sensitive refund case without escalation
- refund to a new payment method
